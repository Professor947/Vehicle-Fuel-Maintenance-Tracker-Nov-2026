# M3 manual test checklist (Android device, dev build)

Setup
- [ ] Apply `supabase/migrations/20261108000000_wash_service.sql` (adds "wash").
- [ ] Rebuild the dev client (new native module: expo-notifications).

"Any record?" prompts
- [ ] Add a bike → app opens Maintenance listing Oil, Filter, Chain oil, General service, Wash; a car has no Chain oil.
- [ ] Each row without a record asks "Any record of the last …?"; Yes opens Log service with the type
      preselected; after saving, the row shows "Last: <date> at <km>".
- [ ] No → question disappears and never comes back (also after app restart); no popup or nagging.

Defaults and due dates (new bike, odometer 10,000)
- [ ] Oil: "Every 1,500 km or 60 days, whichever first · Default"; Chain oil 500 km / 14 days;
      Filter 3,000 km / 180 days; General service 30 days; Wash 14 days. Car: Oil 3,500/90, Filter 5,000/180,
      General service 60 days, Wash 14 days.
- [ ] Before any refuels: hint "Log refuels or odometer readings to predict km-based dates".
- [ ] Log odometer readings over a few days (e.g. +40 km/day) → km-based due dates appear; "km left" decreases.
- [ ] Drive past due km (odometer reading) → status "Overdue" (red) and "N km over".
- [ ] Within 100 km or 1 day of due → "Due soon" (amber). Home Maintenance card lists due items first.

Observed intervals
- [ ] Log two oil changes (e.g. 1,200 km and 40 days apart) → Oil shows "Based on your history", every 1,200 km or 40 days.
- [ ] Wash logged twice 10 days apart → "Every 10 days · Based on your history" (still no km).

Rule editor (tap a row)
- [ ] Custom: 2,000 km, days empty → "Every 2,000 km · Custom". Both empty → "Enter km, days, or both".
- [ ] Turn "Remind me" off → row shows "Reminders off"; no notification for it.
- [ ] Back to Automatic → defaults/observed again. Rules sync to Supabase `reminder_rules`.

Notifications
- [ ] First run asks for notification permission (Android 13+). Deny → app works, no reminders.
- [ ] Set a custom wash interval of 1 day, log a wash now → a notification arrives ~now (1 day before due).
- [ ] An already-overdue item notifies once; reopening the app does not repeat it.
- [ ] Logging the service resets it; the next due gets its own reminder.
- [ ] With ≥2 refuels: "Refuel soon — <plate>" arrives 1 day before the predicted refuel date.
- [ ] Kill the app / airplane mode: scheduled notifications still arrive.
- [ ] Urdu: notification text and Maintenance screen in Urdu.
